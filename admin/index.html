Content Helper

This page helps you build a correctly formatted block of content to paste into data/roadmap-data.json or jobs/jobs-data.js — without having to hand-type JSON yourself. It does not save, upload, or publish anything. Nothing you type here leaves your browser, and this page cannot connect to GitHub or change the live site on its own.

🔒 Everything on this page runs locally in your browser. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no server involved.
